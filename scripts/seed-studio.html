4 Students

Creates verified accounts that can sign in, and enrols them in the selected class. Re-running is safe — existing emails are reused, not duplicated.

5 Syllabus & question bank

A pack is a whole subject → module → chapter → section → concept tree plus its questions, imported as active and verified so the teacher can pick them in the question bank and the test generator straight away.

Add a single node to the tree

6 Sit the exam

Author and schedule the paper in the app as the teacher, then come back here to have the class attempt it. Each student gets a session, answers, and a real submit — objective questions are graded instantly, subjective ones go to the AI evaluator.

Needs npm run dev:worker running, plus the Flask engine on EVAL_ENGINE_URL. Without the worker the papers just queue.
